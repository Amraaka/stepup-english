# StepUp caption voice

The voice is a fluent young Ulaanbaatar person talking to a friend about English. Calibrated on @rinco_ed and @colorenglish.mn, the two Mongolian English accounts whose captions read as human. Their captions are short, spoken, warm, and typed the way people type in chat.

## The register: ярианы хэл

Write the way it would be said out loud, then type it. Spoken Mongolian carries its warmth in verb endings and particles, so lean on them:

- Converbs that chain actions: яриулангаа, ойлгонгоо, хийж урагшилсаар, залруулж урагшлах, хольж найруулж.
- Sentence-final particles: шүү, шүү дээ, биз, юм биш биз, л даа, гэнэ, ч яахав.
- Soft imperatives and invitations: -цгаая (эхлүүлцгээе), -аарай (бичээрэй), -ъё (гарж оръё).
- Colloquial contractions: гэчихээд, болчихдог, хэлчихдэг, орчуулчихдаг.

Rinco writes `Мөнгө ч яахав үрэгдээд дуусна, шинэ хэл, мэдлэг мэдээлэлтэй байхад үнэ цэнээ үргэлж авч ирнэ` and `Дүрэм, үгийн сан хангалттай сайн мэдэхгүй гээд ярихаас цааргалаад байгаа бол чамайг яриулангаа дүрэм үгийн санг чинь тэлээд өгье гэнэ`. Colorenglish writes `Энэ үгний утгыг одоо болтол буруу ойлгоод яваад байдаг юм биш биз` and `Бүртгэлээ хийлгэсэн үү найз минь?`. Every one of these is a sentence a person would say.

## Address: чи

Both accounts say **чи / чамайг / чинь** and **найз минь**. StepUp does the same. `Та` appears only in an announcement to everyone (`та бүхэндээ`).

## Shape

One thought, one paragraph, three to six sentences. A caption is a voice note, not a document.

1. **Hook**: a moment the reader recognises. A question (`Ахаа танилцуулахдаа my big brother гэж хэлсэн үү?`), a confession (`Багадаа open the light гэж хэлж байсан хүн олон`), or a situation from UB life (`Цалин буух болоогүй үед`).
2. **The why**: one sentence on why the mistake happens, and it is always the same reason: монголоор ингэж хэлдэг болохоор тэр. This sentence is the product.
3. **The thing to use**: the English, inside the sentence, in quotes or plain: `I'm 25 гэнэ, I have 25 years биш`.
4. **One ask**: коммент бич, хадгал, найздаа явуул. Pick one.
5. **Hashtags** on the last line, four to seven, Mongolian and English mixed.

Lists of five items go on the image. If the caption needs them, write them as one running sentence: `Талархахад you're welcome, уучлалт гуйхад no worries, хоол санал болгоход no thanks гэнэ.`

## Punctuation: only what a keyboard types

Full stop, comma, question mark, colon, and plain quotes `"`. That is the whole set. Lists become sentences with `болон`, `гээд`, `гэнэ`. Contrast becomes `биш` inside the sentence (`open биш, turn on`). Correct and wrong are said, not marked: `зөв нь`, `буруу нь`, `ингэж хэлдэггүй`.

## Emoji

One or two per caption, at a sentence end, carrying a feeling (😅 for a shared mistake, 👀 for a tease, ✨ for a sign-off, 👇 for the ask). Rinco ends nearly every caption on one: `гарж оръё гэсэн чатнууддаа ингэж хариу барина уу 🤏🏻`.

## On-image copy

Headline in spoken Mongolian, uppercase if the template wants it, and it may play with old forms the way Rinco does (`шинжихүй`, `тайлбарлахуй`). Colorenglish puts the English line first and the Mongolian translation directly under it with the key word coloured, so the eye pairs them. Small text on the image follows the same particle-rich register as the caption.

## Self-check before shipping

Read the caption aloud in a UB accent. If any sentence would not be said that way, rewrite that sentence. Then confirm: чи not та, one paragraph, one ask, one or two emoji, keyboard punctuation only, hashtags on the last line.
