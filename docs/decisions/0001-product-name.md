# 0001 — Product name: StepUp English

Date: 2026-08-25
Status: decided

Named **StepUp English** — matches the step-by-step (small by small) learning philosophy, clear to both kids and parents, easy to say and spell in Mongolia.

Rejected: Easy English (taken as "Easy English Mongolia"), cultural names (Naadam English etc. — felt cringy), pure Mongolian names (wanted "English" in the name).
