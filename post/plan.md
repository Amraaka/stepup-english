# 10-day plan v2 — 2026-09-15 → 09-24

Format study of the six biggest English-learning accounts (28M followers combined: @english.grammar.vocab, @bbclearningenglish, @learnenglishwithclemence, @englishbygiovana, @englishfuns, @englishwithlucy) shows the same handful of formats carrying almost every viral post. v2 uses those formats. Every post is universal English, explained in Mongolian; no "Mongolian mistake" framing except where noted.

Three posts a day. 08:00 save-worthy list · 12:30 quiz/participate · 20:30 real situation or ladder.
Templates: `templates/social-studio.html`. Voice: `.claude/skills/stepup-caption-voice/SKILL.md`. Guide: `templates/GUIDE.md`.

## Rules carried into every post
- Mongolian gloss under every English line (colorenglish style: English first, Mongolian directly under, key word coloured).
- The "why this is better" is one sentence in the caption, spoken register, чи.
- Quizzes never reveal the answer on the image; the answer goes in the next day's Story.
- Lists are 6–10 items; more than 10 goes to a carousel.
